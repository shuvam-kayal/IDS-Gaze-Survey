# Privacy and consent baseline

This is engineering guidance, not legal advice or a substitute for ethics review. Explain purpose, data types, duration, processing, retention, sharing, risks and withdrawal before collection. Research consent and camera permission are separate. Camera is opt-in with visible status/stop control; stop on withdrawal or session end. Prefer transient/on-device frame handling and do not persist raw video/images by default. Minimize identifiers; use pseudonymous IDs, role restrictions, TLS in deployment and audit sensitive actions. Define export/deletion, retention, backup expiry and derived-data deletion before recruiting real participants. Never log raw answers, frames, tokens or sensitive payloads by default. Fixtures must be synthetic or appropriately consented/de-identified. Before deployment add threat model, dependency scan, origin allowlist, rate limits, encryption, restore test and incident process.
